Privacy Policy for Lede

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Lede is an RSS reader for Android. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary

Lede does not collect, transmit or store any personal data. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Everything the app knows stays on your device.

What Lede stores on your device

  • The feed URLs you add, and any categories you create
  • Articles downloaded from those feeds
  • Which articles you have read, bookmarked or marked as favourite
  • Your theme preference (system, light or dark)

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, backed up to our servers, or shared with anyone, because there are no servers. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can export a copy at any time from the Feeds tab.

Network connections the app makes

Lede connects to the internet only to do the things you ask it to:

  • Fetching feeds you subscribed to. When you add a feed or refresh, the app requests that feed's URL directly from the publisher's server.
  • Loading an article. When you open an article whose feed only supplied a summary, the app requests that article's page directly from the publisher.
  • Opening a link. Tapping a link opens it in your browser.

These requests go straight from your phone to the publisher. They do not pass through any server operated by us. Those publishers will see your IP address and request, exactly as they would if you visited the site in a browser.

What Lede does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in or registration
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No location access
  • No contacts, camera, microphone or photo access
  • No third-party tracking SDKs of any kind
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected

Permissions

The release build declares:

  • Internet and network state — required to fetch the feeds you subscribe to.
  • Read/write external storage, limited to Android 12L and older — used only when you choose a file to import or save a backup you asked for.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or permission to draw over other apps.

Children

Lede is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes
